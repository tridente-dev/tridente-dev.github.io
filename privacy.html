Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 — Pursuant to Art. 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

1. Data Controller

The data controller is Giancarlo Tridente, based in Milan (MI), Italy. Contactable at g.tridente.web@gmail.com.

2. Data Processed and Purposes

  • Contact Form: name, email, and message content, provided voluntarily. They are used to respond to the request and, if requested by the data subject, to carry out pre-contractual activities.
  • Technical Data: the hosting service may process IP addresses, logs, and device data to deliver the pages and protect the infrastructure. The hosting provider's privacy policies also apply to such processing.
  • Local Preferences: the site uses the browser's LocalStorage to remember the selected theme and language. These preferences remain in the browser and are not sent via the form. Users can delete these preferences at any time by clearing site data in their browser.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The site uses the browser's LocalStorage exclusively to store certain user preferences, such as the graphic theme and the selected language. This information remains stored on the user's device and is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking purposes.

The main portfolio code does not implement analytics, advertising, or profiling tools. All fonts used by the site are self-hosted; therefore, no external font services (such as Google Fonts) are loaded.

Some demonstration pages included in the portfolio may load JavaScript libraries or other resources from third-party CDN services, including Tailwind CSS. When such resources are requested, the respective providers may receive technical data necessary for the connection, such as the IP address and information relating to the user's browser and device.

Loading resources from CDNs does not automatically entail the use of profiling cookies or the obligation to request consent. The need for any additional notices or prior consent is assessed based on the tools actually used, their purposes, and the applicable legislation.

Should cookies or other tracking tools not strictly necessary be introduced in the future, their use will be subject to the user's prior consent, where required by applicable law, through any consent management tools.

For further information on the processing of personal data, please consult this privacy policy.

4. Legal Basis for Processing and Transfers Outside the EEA

4.1 Legal Basis

  • Pre-contractual requests (quote or collaboration): processing is necessary to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Generic information requests: processing is based on the legitimate interest of the controller in replying to and managing communications, where the conditions of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR are met.
  • Site security and operation: processing is based on the legitimate interest in ensuring the security of the site and preventing abuse, where applicable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Web3Forms declares that such transfers are governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) approved by the European Commission.

  • Web3Forms Privacy Policy — official documentation of the provider.
  • The safeguards applied are the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) adopted by the European Commission (Decision 2021/914/EU), as declared by the provider.

5. Recipients and Providers

  • GitHub Pages: site hosting and possible processing of technical logs. Consult the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Web3Forms: The contact form uses the Web3Forms service (Web3Creative) to forward data. Web3Forms acts as a Data Processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The service is based in India and uses infrastructure (AWS, Cloudflare) that may involve the transfer of data outside the EEA, governed as described in section 4.2. Data sent via the form is retained for a maximum of 3 years. Consult the Web3Forms Privacy Policy and the provider's updated contractual documentation.

6. Retention Periods

Data are retained for the time strictly necessary for the purposes described above, according to the following criteria:

  • Data in the email inbox: up to 24 months from the last exchange of communications, unless further retention is required for the defence of legal claims or for legal obligations.
  • Quote requests: up to 24 months from the request, in line with the statute of limitations applicable to pre-contractual relationships.
  • Data retained by Web3Forms: according to the retention times declared by the service (currently up to 3 years), after which the data are deleted.
  • LocalStorage preferences (theme and language): until deletion by the user through the browser settings.
