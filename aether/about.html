SINCE 2018 / MILAN

We design silence for your desk.

AETHER was born from the idea that the objects around us shape the way we think. We work with Italian artisans to create pieces that last a lifetime.

01 / Materials

Only honest matter

Recycled aluminum, local ceramic, mouth-blown glass. No plastic, no toxic coating.

02 / Production

Handmade in Italy

We collaborate with 12 artisans across Lombardy, Veneto and Tuscany. Each piece is individually inspected.

03 / Philosophy

Less, but better

We publish few collections a year. We prefer longevity over novelty, substance over appearance.

Our studio

In our Milan workshop we design each piece starting from a daily gesture: placing a pen, turning on a light, tidying a cable.

We believe design should not shout. It should accompany. That's why our objects have clean lines, calibrated weights and finishes that improve over time.
